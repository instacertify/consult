Who must register

Who needs an LMPC certificate

The LMPC certificate, also called the Legal Metrology certificate, is required under Rule 27 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 by anyone who manufactures, packs or imports pre-packaged goods for sale in India. If your product reaches the buyer in a sealed pack, it applies to you.

LMPC certificate for importers

Importer registration is required before pre-packaged goods are cleared for home consumption. Customs commonly asks for the certificate number at the bill-of-entry stage.

Manufacturer LMPC registration

Anyone manufacturing pre-packaged commodities needs manufacturer registration, with the declarations on each pack matching what is filed.

Packer registration & packing license

Legal Metrology packer registration covers businesses that pack goods made by someone else, including private-label and contract packing. Often asked for as a packing license.

E-commerce sellers & brands

Products listed on marketplaces must carry the prescribed declarations, and the entity packing or importing them must hold a valid registration.

Weight & measurement certificate

Model approval certificate, importer registration, dealer permit and repairer license for weights, measures and weighing instruments. All handled by the same team.

FMCG, pharma & chemicals

Sector-specific declaration rules on net quantity, MRP, batch and consumer-care details — checked against your artwork, not just your paperwork.

One registration, all of India

Issued centrally. Not state by state.

This is the part most businesses, and a fair few consultants, get wrong. The LMPC certificate is issued centrally by the Director of Legal Metrology under the Department of Consumer Affairs. Your state Controller is sent a copy for information. They do not issue it.

Which means one registration covers you across India. You do not need a separate certificate for every state you sell into, and you do not need a consultant sitting in your city to get it. If someone has told you otherwise, ask them which authority signs the certificate.

Filed with the Director

Application goes to the Director of Legal Metrology under Rule 27 — the central authority that signs and issues the certificate.

Your state is notified

A copy goes to the Controller of Legal Metrology in your state for information, so local enforcement can see your registration on record.

Why it matters

The cost of getting this wrong is not the consultant's fee

Two things go wrong for importers. The consignment sits at port while the paperwork is sorted out — demurrage, detention and a delayed launch. Or goods reach the market with declarations that don't match what was filed, and the penalty follows later.

Both are avoidable at the filing stage. That's why we audit the label before submission rather than after a query.

Penalties under the Act

Where a pre-packaged commodity does not conform to the declarations on the package, the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 provides for a fine of up to ₹25,000 for a first offence, up to ₹50,000 for a second, and ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 and/or imprisonment up to one year for subsequent offences.

Separate penalties apply where net quantity errors exceed the prescribed limits.

Do I need an LMPC certificate if I only import?

Yes. An importer of pre-packaged commodities intended for sale in India needs registration under Rule 27 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. Customs commonly asks for the registration details at the bill-of-entry stage, which is why getting it in place before the consignment lands matters.

Call us rather than filling in the form — we'll get a specialist on it immediately. We can't shorten the department's processing time, but we can make sure the application is filed correctly and same-day, and that nothing in your documents or label triggers a query that adds another week.

What's the difference between LMPC and Legal Metrology registration?

They're the same thing in everyday use. "LMPC" stands for Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) — the registration made under Rule 27 of those Rules. Separately, the Legal Metrology Act also covers weights and measuring instruments, which need a model approval certificate, dealer or repairer licences. We handle both.

Is it cheaper to buy LMPC bundled with EPR or BIS?

Only if you actually need those registrations. A package that includes LMPC "free" alongside EPR is good value when your product genuinely requires both — and poor value when it doesn't. How long is the certificate valid, and do you handle renewal?

There is no expiry date and no annual renewal to file. The registration holds good for as long as your details stay the same.

What does need a fresh filing is a change: adding products that aren't on your commodity list, moving or adding premises, or a change in the entity's name or constitution. Tell us when something changes and we'll file the amendment. We keep your file, so we already have everything.

Do I need a separate LMPC certificate for each state?

No. The certificate is issued centrally by the Director of Legal Metrology, and a copy goes to the Controller in your state for information. One registration covers you across India.
